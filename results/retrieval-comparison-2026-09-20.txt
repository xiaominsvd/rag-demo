Takeaways
================================================================
- This time all three methods retrieve chunk-6, so all three answers
  converge on "Chroma". The methods differ only in noise chunks and
  ordering -- a much less dramatic split than the previous Chinese-docs
  run.
- The crispest selection rule ("Under 1M vectors on a single machine:
  Chroma or FAISS is enough.") lives in chunk-7, which NONE of the three
  methods retrieved. Why? A chunking artifact: the "## Selection Advice"
  header landed at the tail of chunk-6 while its body became chunk-7, so
  the advice chunk has almost no shared vocabulary with the question.
  Lesson: header-aware chunking (keep a header with its body) matters as
  much as the retrieval algorithm.
- Vector and hybrid returned the identical chunk set here; hybrid's only
  effect was promoting chunk-6 to rank 1 via the rank swap (BM25 #1 +
  vector #3 beats vector #2 + BM25 #2 by 0.00001).
- BM25 still drags in an off-topic chunk (chunk-12), same weakness as
  before: lexical match without semantic understanding.
