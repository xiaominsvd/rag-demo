Takeaways
================================================================
- Pure vector: clear answer (hit chunk-3), but one off-topic chunk
  (chunk-1) wastes context.
- Pure BM25: thin, hedged answer -- nothing factually wrong, but the key
  selection recommendation is missing. The user cannot tell information
  is missing from the answer; this is the most insidious symptom of weak
  retrieval.
- Hybrid RRF: union of both sides -- kept chunk-3 from the vector side
  and chunk-4 from the BM25 side, dropped the off-topic chunk-1. Most
  complete answer.
- Everything else in the three prompts is identical; the three chunks
  stuffed into the context set the ceiling for answer quality:
  garbage in, garbage out.
